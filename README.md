# Solid Credit

Understand your credit in plain English with your Solid Credit account.

## What you can do

- Check your Equifax VantageScore 3.0 credit score and what is helping or hurting it.
- See the accounts, hard inquiries, and records on your credit reports, with likely report errors and recent credit activity.
- Get your custom Credit Plan, with the next best thing to do.
- Check your rent and bill reporting status and the next steps to set it up.
- Check on your Solid Credit account: your membership, your disputes, and your Identity Protection access and restoration case status.

Access is read-only. Claude can explain your information and link you back to Solid Credit, but it cannot file disputes, change reporting, or modify your account. Available information depends on your membership and the permissions you approve.

New to Solid Credit? You can create an account when you connect.

## Try asking

- What's helping and hurting my credit?
- What should I work on next in my Credit Plan?
- Are there likely errors on my credit reports?

## Install

In Claude Code:

```
/plugin install solid-credit --marketplace SolidCredit/claude-plugin
```

In Claude on the web or desktop, open Customize > Plugins > Add > Add marketplace and enter `SolidCredit/claude-plugin`.

## Connect

The plugin adds the Solid Credit connector (`https://app.solidcredit.com/api/mcp`). Connect it, sign in to Solid Credit, and approve what Claude can read. To disconnect, open Profile → Applications in Solid Credit and select Disconnect.
